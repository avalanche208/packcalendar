Android subscription instructions

If your calendar app supports calendar subscriptions by URL, paste the address below into its subscription option.

For apps without that feature, ICSx⁵ subscribes to the feed and keeps events synced into Android’s calendar storage. You can then view them in Samsung Calendar or another app that displays device calendars. No Google account is required. ICSx⁵ is a separate app and may cost money.

In ICSx⁵, add a subscription, paste this site's subscription address, and select a refresh interval. Enable the subscribed calendar in your preferred calendar app.

Apple & other calendar apps

Apple: Tap the Apple Calendar link and confirm Subscribe. If your browser shows an import prompt, cancel it and open this page in Safari. You should see a subscription prompt. You can also open Calendar → Calendars → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar, then paste the address below.

Other apps: Choose Subscribe from web or Add calendar by URL. Importing a downloaded file creates a one-time copy and will not receive changes.

The subscription is read-only. Updates appear as your app refreshes; they may not be immediate.
